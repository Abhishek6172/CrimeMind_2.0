## Case summary
A university-owned research laptop was reported missing from Room 204 after an afternoon project meeting. Several staff members and students used the room during the day. The available records provide a partial timeline, but there is not enough evidence to identify a person responsible.

## Known facts
- The laptop was reportedly seen on the meeting-room desk at approximately 15:50.
- The room was used for a project meeting between 16:00 and 16:45.
- The reporting person noticed the laptop missing at approximately 18:25.
- The electronic access log records three entries after the meeting.
- A witness recalls seeing someone carrying a dark laptop sleeve, but could not identify the person or confirm the device inside it.

## Persons to clarify / interview
